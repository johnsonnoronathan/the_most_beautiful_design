Notes toward a beautiful design

Beauty is mostly what you leave out.

01

Leave room

Space is not what is missing. It is what lets the eye rest, and the one important thing be found.

02

One colour that matters

A single accent, spent only where it means something. Everything else stays quiet.

Aa
03

Type that whispers

Light weights, open tracking, few sizes. Words that ask to be read rather than demand it.

04

Slow time

Motion that eases in and lingers. Nothing hurries; nothing blinks for attention.

05

Honest surfaces

Flat colour, true edges. No glow pretending to be light, no gloss pretending to be glass.

06

Stop early

Finished is not when nothing more can be added, but when nothing is left to take away. After Saint-Exupéry.
