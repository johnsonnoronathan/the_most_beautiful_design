Afterglow Studies

One valley · eighteen ridgelines

Design studies for the Afterglow film: one generated mountain valley, drawn from code and lit by the hour. Every page can grow a new valley from a seed.

  • Study No. 02 · Poster1080 × 1350 Afterglow

    The minutes after sunset, with the real stages of twilight on the side.
  • Interactive1440 × 900 A day of light

    Scrub or play 24 hours over the valley: sun, moon, stars and ten lighting moods.
  • Study No. 03 · Series2200 × 1080 One valley, four hours

    First light, noon, golden hour and blue hour. Nothing moves but the light.
